\begin{frame}
    \frametitle{Conclusion}
    \small

    \textbf{Takeaways}
    \begin{itemize}
        \item The Morris decision created a sharp institutional change in New York City's land-use process
        \item The post-reform system gave district-level politics a larger role in discretionary approvals
        \item Preliminary evidence suggests housing production shifted away from homeowner-heavy districts
    \end{itemize}

    \vspace{0.25em}
    \textbf{Next steps}
    \begin{itemize}
        \item Refine pre-period \(H_j\) construction and robustness checks
        \item Add richer controls for baseline development potential
        \item Clarify whether results are driven by larger buildings and discretionary review channels
    \end{itemize}
\end{frame}
