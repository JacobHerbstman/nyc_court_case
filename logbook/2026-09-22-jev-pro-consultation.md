## Main recommendations

1. Clarify the measured concepts before further tuning. Displacement should concern existing residents' or businesses' occupancy or ability to remain, including expressly described indirect pressure. General economic harm, nuisance, buffering, or protection of residential amenities does not establish displacement. An explicit no-displacement finding still counts as discussion.
2. Preserve affirmative preservation goals separately from expressed concerns or requests. Supporting a rezoning because it preserves character is not automatically an objection or request. Explicitly requesting a rezoning for that purpose can count as a request. Preserve the existing broad Council-involvement definition, including concerns that prompted a proposal; distinguish that from the narrower review-concern field.
3. Separate a specific accepted undertaking or imposed substantive condition from approval of the main proposal. Neither a new revision nor causation by opposition is necessary for an undertaking. Section 8 commitments and genuine design agreements can qualify. Approval alone and generic plan-conformity clauses do not establish a particular obligation. Record the decision-making body and action without inferring later Council action or implementation.
4. Separate the substantive existence question from selection of supporting pages. Pro proposed explicit yes, no, ambiguous, and context-missing choices for obligations, followed by evidence selection and a different, focused verification question using the selected passage and necessary context. A selected page is an evidence window, not an extracted quotation. Rejecting one selected passage does not establish report-wide absence.
5. Judge civic organizations by their role in the focal project. An applicant or co-sponsor is not an independent civic participant merely because it is a nonprofit. Similarly named groups must remain distinct unless the source establishes identity.

The proposed obligation question begins: "Does the supplied text establish at least one specific applicant undertaking or individualized substantive condition imposed by a deciding body concerning [topic]?" It excludes main-proposal approval alone, proposed features without acceptance, generic compliance, and generic plan conformity, while permitting prior and explicitly conditional undertakings. This is a proposed replacement meaning, not a silent reinterpretation of historical `adopted` labels.

## Implications for disputed examples

For C 830464 ZMX, the supplied passages support design, traffic, and environmental conditions but do not establish displacement. The topic assignment needs correction without denying that binding conditions exist.

For C 050480 ZMX, Pro rejected treating all six disputed concern/adoption judgments as obvious Jev errors. The excerpts establish preservation goals and a zoning decision. They do not by themselves establish separate specific obligations. The collective statement attributed to all speakers can include the Councilmember, but its preservation rationale is not necessarily a review concern. Character and scale requests require checking the contextual link between the community's request and the cited plan. These are proposed adjudications under revised definitions; the original references remain unchanged.

The resident-versus-organization fix in C 180346 PSX remains a demonstrated wording improvement. C 900038 PLQ demonstrates a source repair. The clear Council-concern and boilerplate target cases still need successful Jev answers; Pro's interpretation of their excerpts does not fill those missing model results.

## Proposed bounded next test

Pro recommended 20 reports: ten known diagnostic or positive-control reports and ten fresh, source-eligible reports, split equally between short and long multipart packets. Compare the current candidate with one frozen revised question bundle plus prescribed evidence verification. Reuse only exactly identical successful requests; do not repeat successful calls for voting. Preserve screening answers separately from verification results.

Freeze definitions, source hashes, segmentation, selection, evaluation mappings, and verification rules before examining results. Report positive detection, false positives, precision, resolved coverage, evidence validity, and source/service missingness separately. Mark definition changes explicitly rather than pooling them into a misleading before/after accuracy total. A stronger reader's resolution of an escalated case is not a Jev success. Twenty reports can test the proposed distinctions and preserve positive controls, but cannot establish corpus-wide accuracy.

Our assessment: the core distinction and evidence-check recommendations are useful and compatible with preserving prior work. Pro's proposed release thresholds are suggestions, not agreed research standards. In particular, the user's tolerance for some mistakes should inform any eventual release rule. The next test should establish whether narrower definitions and verification improve useful coverage, rather than merely increase unresolved answers.
