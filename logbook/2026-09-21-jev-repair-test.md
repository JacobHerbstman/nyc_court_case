Two continuing problems are visible in the available answers. C 050480 ZMX still disagrees with the reader about character-related concerns and adoption. C 830464 ZMX still treats economic effects on neighboring homeowners and physical buffers as displacement concerns and protections. The selected passages discuss land-use compatibility and buffering, without establishing eviction or relocation. The displacement wording's broad reference to protection of existing residents appears to admit a wider meaning than intended.

On the fresh C 050480 ZMX case, Jev disagrees with a reader who treats a statement attributed to all hearing speakers, including the Councilmember, as a review concern. This is a collective attribution and a definitional boundary; the report's other Council fields already identify involvement. C 010691 ZSM, the boilerplate adoption test, and the repaired Queens case also remain unavailable. These omissions prevent a claim that the central remaining fixes passed.

Seven development cases and four fresh reports were selected before examining new answers. The fresh sample uses a fixed seed, excludes prior human and source-audit reports, and is limited to completed reports with resolved page scope and at most two parts. Two Sol-medium readers each coded two full corrected packets without Jev answers. Their references were frozen before the manager inspected new answers; requests could be in flight while the readings were finalized. Original human coding is unchanged. These are development checks and fallible AI comparisons, not an estimate of corpus-wide accuracy.

Before restarting it, the unreturned Council and boilerplate cases need valid answers, and displacement protection needs a narrower definition.
