The reports do not support a blanket preference for Tyler's differing codes.
Source review covers 204 differing field values across 51 reports. Under the
working definition that permits substantive CPC or local discussion of an issue,
156 rulings retain Jacob's value, 43 use Tyler's, and five remain unresolved.
Restricting issue coding to local actors changes these counts to 142, 57, and
five. The conclusion therefore survives that definition choice. These are
results for selected disagreements, not accuracy estimates for either coder.
Of the 204 differences, 184 have both readings marked complete; the other 20
involve provisional Tyler coding. The generated appendix separates those groups.

The distinction matters especially for the two fields that motivated the review.
Among 21 character/scale/preservation differences, the broad reading supports
Jacob in 13 and Tyler in eight. Among 21 revision/concession differences, it
supports Jacob in 18 and Tyler in three. Many retained positives have concrete
local conditions, documented changes, or substantive binding commitments.
Some corrected positives instead describe routine permit findings, generic
project benefits, or a study without an adopted substantive change.

For example, Tyler's zero is better supported for character in C 170382 ZSM:
the streetscape statement applies a routine special-permit finding, rather than
developing a concrete character issue (PDF pp. 5–6). His zero for revision in
C 140316 ZSR is also supported: the declaration requires an archaeological
study, and the codebook excludes studies alone (p. 6). Conversely, Jacob's
revision and explicit-response positives in C 150110 ZSM have direct support:
the applicant moved bicycle parking from the cellar to the ground floor and
revised the drawings following the borough president's comments (p. 7).
Jacob's revision positive for C 810498 ZMK is documented in the incorporated
C 810535 HDK report: the urban-renewal/disposition application was revised to
map a street, close part of another, and add the streetbed to the supermarket
project (companion p. 1). Companion evidence can therefore matter even when the
focal report is short. Each recorded ruling retains its source and page.

The working revision field continues to include substantive commitments or
conditions; it is not a measure of new concessions caused by local opposition.
The timing and actor described in the evidence remain essential to that later
research question. A local request alone does not establish an applicant
response or revision. Similarly, a routine favorable character finding does
not establish a character concern. These boundaries were applied during this
review rather than inferring that every human or model positive was correct.

Four unresolved opposition differences concern dissenting Community Board
votes without stated substantive grounds for those votes: C 970578 ZMX,
C 190072 ZSK, C 210173 HAK, and C 140181 ZMM. An any-dissent measure and a
substantive-opposition measure answer different questions. These are left blank
in the working reconciled field pending a codebook choice; their original
values and vote evidence remain. C 240369 ZMM is also unresolved because the
opposing business representative's status as a local actor is not established.
These cases do not justify dropping any report. Broad issue scope is a stated
working assumption, not a decision confirmed by Jacob. Both local and broad
readings are saved; 14 disputed fields depend on that choice.

Three Sol-medium readers reviewed the complete supplied page-marked report
bundles, both coders' values and notes, and the coding definitions. Two readers
started fresh; one continued an earlier source-review session. Across the 51
bundles, 316,026 source words were supplied, including repeated companion text.
The manager reviewed the rulings, checked key passages, and requested corrections
to quotations and interpretation boundaries. The frozen submissions reflect
that feedback, rather than untouched first responses. Two later manager notes
harmonize bare-vote cases with the other unresolved definition mismatches.
This was unblinded, AI-assisted reconciliation. It is not an independent human
validation exercise, and the same model family should not receive an improved
accuracy claim simply for agreeing with these rulings. No paid API calls or
new manual reading by Jacob or Tyler were required.

The reconciled table retains all 7,033 original field records covering 340
human-coded reports and appends a working value with provenance. It applies
199 resolved rulings and leaves the five reviewed ambiguities blank. Original
agreements and single-coder values are carried forward with their original
status, not described as newly validated. Both original coder columns are
unchanged. The older combined development-direction field is excluded from
direct comparison with the newer split definitions. Two nonstandard civic-group
values are interpreted from source evidence while retaining the literal original
category. The full ZAP project universe and historical pilot comparisons remain
unchanged.

Reproduction uses branch `cpc_llm_training`, based on commit `6077733` with the
current uncommitted pipeline work, the existing corpus vintage, and the
September 15 human-source snapshot and saved adjudications. The source ledger
lives in the output of `record_ulurp_cpc_training_labels`; the review protocol,
assignments, source hashes, and submitted judgments are archived under
`data_raw/cpc_human_coding/reconciliation_20260915/`. Run `make` in the `code`
folders of that task, `summarize_text_cpc_trends`, and the regex-label audit.
Then run `make output/2026-09-15-cpc-human-reconciliation.pdf` from `logbook`.
Make applies recorded judgments and rebuilds the generated appendix; it never
launches model readers. Reproducibility means rebuilding from those saved
judgments, not guaranteeing identical future model answers.

The build verifies exact coverage of current disagreements, unchanged original
values, source-bundle membership, source-text hashes, and quotations on their
cited pages. Matching text establishes provenance rather than interpretation
accuracy. Original human, regex, and frozen v3 pilot files retain their prior
hashes. A separate reconstruction checks the local-scope alternative without
changing the official broad-scope output. Corrupted keys and evidence are
rejected before replacement labels are written.
