title: "A frozen comparison of Jev questions and evidence checks"
date: "September 22, 2026"
---

The experiment does not establish that the refined questions improve extraction. Jev completed ten of twenty reports with the baseline questions and four with the revised questions; only three reports completed in both conditions. None of the revised long reports completed. Of 85 distinct new screening requests, 23 succeeded after the single permitted recovery, while 62 remained unavailable. Nine exact prior successes were reused. The failures were received HTTP 503 responses, kept separate from coding errors. All nine smaller evidence-window requests eventually succeeded. Observed account spending remained $0 with $5 available; the higher completion rate for small windows is descriptive and does not identify why screening requests failed.

The evidence checker also revealed a defect in our implementation. Its request construction copied the substantive instruction string but omitted the field's original true/false criteria. For civic fields, those criteria contain the explicit exclusion of Community Boards, government agencies and applicants. As a result, the second-stage prompt did not transmit the full definition that the first stage and source readers used. The twenty-pair evidence audit agrees with Jev in ten cases, with five false establishments and five false rejections relative to the fallible reader. Four of the five false establishments are civic fields. These numbers describe the defective requests, not the intended complete evidence rule. The original requests and results are preserved; the checker is not promoted to production. The next implementation correction is to carry the original substantive answer criteria into the focused question as well as its instruction text, under a new recorded request vintage.

The small available fresh sample also supplies a concrete semantic failure beyond that omission. In C 190426 PCX, Bronx Community Board 9 recommends approval with on-site parking and traffic-compliance conditions on page 4. The CPC resolution on page 6 approves the acquisition without adopting those conditions. Both revised screening and verification nevertheless mark a traffic/parking obligation. The checker does reject a neighborhood-character concern inferred from the same report's suitability discussion; the final field stays unresolved rather than becoming a report-wide negative. It does not recover the two missed design and environmental undertakings involving specifically promised tree planting. The original environmental reference cites routine tree-pit maintenance, a weak rationale under this codebook; the same page's explicit landscaped-buffer promise provides an alternative substantive basis. This later source check is interpretation, not a replacement of the frozen reference.

Among the 44 mapped primary fields in the two fresh reports completed by revised screening, screening detects one of three reference positives, gives two false positives among 41 reference negatives, and resolves all 44 fields. After verification, one of three positives is detected, four false positives remain, and one field is unresolved. The three new goal fields per report are all negative in these two reports. Across the two completed controls, the checker adds another false civic answer. Across the 26 mapped primary judgments available in both conditions, it corrects none and turns two previously correct answers into errors. These selected and service-limited denominators cannot establish population accuracy or broad before/after improvement. The fifty target fields across the two fresh reports resolve 49/50 after verification; across all ten fresh reports, 200/250 target fields are unavailable through screening. Five of those fields also have unresolved references, and one additional answered field remains semantically unresolved. Those missing reports cannot be treated as accurate negatives.

Jacob approved one fixed test after the Pro consultation: ten known controls and ten fresh reports, including long reports. The experiment compares the previous candidate questions with narrower definitions on identical repaired source text. A second, focused question checks whether each selected passage and its adjacent text actually establish the claimed fact. This records the difference between an incorrect judgment, an unresolved interpretation and a missing service response.

The revised definitions preserve both sides of the character distinction. A preservation goal remains an observable field even when nobody raises a concern. A clearly attributed request to initiate or adopt a proposal for a stated preservation purpose can still count as a request. Displacement requires loss of occupancy or ability to remain, including expressly described indirect pressure; nuisance or harm to property values alone is insufficient. Specific undertakings and imposed conditions are separated from approval of the principal land-use action. Original undertakings can qualify without evidence that opposition caused a revision. The eight historical adopted fields therefore map to a changed construct, and the three new goal fields have no historical equivalent.

The ten controls are selected examples with 41 field expectations, not a representative accuracy sample. The fresh sample comprises five reports with one or two request parts and five with three or more, selected by a recorded hash seed among source-ready reports. It excludes previously coded or audited source bundles without requiring a prior successful Jev response. Three Sol-medium readers read the fresh reports without Jev answers or historical labels, and two reports receive a second reading. Four of those 72 double-read fields disagree. Those fields and two additional unclear displacement readings remain unresolved; these are fallible AI references, not definitive human labels. All references were frozen before the manager inspected new Jev answers.

Verification uses one source segment and its immediate retained neighbors within the same PDF, including across screening-part boundaries. Identical windows are combined. A rejected positive anchor becomes unresolved, because failure to support a claim in one window does not prove its absence from the full report. Obligations also require an applicant undertaking or an imposed condition. Twenty routed window/field pairs are selected by a separate fixed seed for a blinded evidence reading. That reading diagnoses excerpt validity and never replaces Jev's answer.

Every original human code, source document and production Jev label remains preserved. The bulk runner stays paused. The frozen protocol permits one initial request and one recovery of an explicitly received server failure; successful requests, unknown transport outcomes and rate limits are not repeated. Requests, responses and account credit checks are saved. An alias rather than immutable model weights identifies the returned Jev version, so exact rerun determinism is not claimed.

Reproduction: run `make` from the existing pilot audit's code directory. From `logbook/`, run:

```sh
make output/2026-09-22-jev-frozen-comparison.pdf
```

Ordinary Make builds use the recorded acquisition and reader files and perform no inference. The appendix is generated from those records. The live code and archived request bodies are the experiment specification; this entry does not promote the candidate to production.
