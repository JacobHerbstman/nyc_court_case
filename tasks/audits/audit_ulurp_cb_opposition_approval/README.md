# Audit ULURP Approval After Community Board Opposition

Compares the share of Community Board-opposed applications that were approved
before and after 2002, citywide and by the paper's 59-community-district
homeowner terciles.

The long series uses the decision stated in each CPC report. A second series
uses final Council dispositions matched by exact ULURP application number; that
series begins in 1998 and therefore has only four pre-2002 years. The Council
series is the more direct test of member deference. The CPC series is broader
descriptive evidence about whether the Commission overrode Community Board
opposition.

Outputs are a summary table, a figure, and a deterministic set of source-linked
cases for checking the text rules.

The transition outputs compare the Vallone baseline (1998-2001), a 2002-2009
transition period, and 2010-2025. They also show annual and trailing three-year
Council approval rates and distinguish outright disapproval from matters that
were withdrawn or filed without adoption. End-of-session filings are retained
as unresolved but excluded from approval rates unless another Council record
documents a substantive decision. The transition CSV retains annual
counts at both the application-action and ZAP-project levels so alternative
transition windows can be calculated directly. The zoomed figure uses projects
to avoid giving companion actions separate weight.
The source-linked case output includes every CB-opposed non-PP application with
a verified Council outcome during 2003-2005, not only the nonadoptions.

For Jacob's 200 completed training reports, the audit uses the committed human
vote counts to preserve the agreed substantive coding of unusual cases where a
Community Board formally approved only after demanding a different project.
